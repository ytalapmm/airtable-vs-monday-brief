Airtable Pricing

Pricing model and current tiers.

Pricing model
  • Per-seat, billed per workspace (not per account)
  • Annual billing is the default published rate — monthly billing costs more
  • Two sales tracks: self-serve (Team/Business) and sales-led (Business/Enterprise Scale)
  • AI credits bundled into every plan, but don't roll over month to month
  • Enterprise Scale has no public price
Pricing gotchas
  • Record limits are per-base, not per-workspace (1K Free / 50K Team / 125K Business / 500K Enterprise)
  • Team bills Commenters and above; Business bills Editors and above only — surprise charges on Team when adding read-only users
  • AI credits don't roll over — unused credits expire monthly
  • A separate legacy Enterprise plan still exists with different features
  • Business is dual-track: self-serve ($45/seat) vs. sales-led (custom) — feature parity not disclosed
  • HyperDB (the flagship AI/scale product) is Enterprise-only — no self-serve path
  • Automation runs hard-cap, not throttle, once limits are hit
